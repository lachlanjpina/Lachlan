```markdown

**Goal:** <what a great outcome looks like, in one line>

## Who's in the room
- <name> — <role> — cares about <x> — last contact <date>

## Context
- <history, open threads, account status>

## Agenda I'd propose
1.

## Questions to ask
-

## Likely objections → responses
-

## After the meeting
- Drop notes in `inbox/` and I'll turn them into follow-ups.
```

Use `context/people.md`, `context/accounts.md`, past briefs, and connectors
(email history, CRM, web research on the company) when available. Commit and push.
